I am a Computational Scientist and Machine Learning practitioner (M.Sc. Computational Science, B.Sc. Astronomy, Institut Teknologi Bandung) dedicated to advancing AI through rigorous mathematical foundations. My core expertise lies in statistical modeling and machine learning—both supervised and unsupervised—focusing on extracting reliable structures from massive, high-dimensional datasets. Driven by a strict first-principles philosophy in both mathematical derivation and scientific coding, I strive to build robust, scalable AI systems that bridge theoretical depth with practical, real-world impact.

My research applies these mathematically grounded approaches to complex astrophysical domains. During my master's research, I developed ENTRAP, a hybrid algorithm integrating density-based clustering with Topological Data Analysis (TDA) to identify open star clusters in Gaia DR3 data. Most recently, I completed the ASPIRE research internship at the Anton Pannekoek Institute for Astronomy (University of Amsterdam), where I built an N-body simulation pipeline to study the dynamics of the Kepler-221 exoplanet system. Ultimately, I aim to continuously improve AI/ML methodologies, overcoming their current limitations to deliver innovative solutions for multidisciplinary challenges.

Anton Pannekoek Institute for Astronomy, University of Amsterdam · Supervised by Dr. T. C. N. Boekholt

Building an N-body simulation pipeline (AMUSE/Huayno) to investigate the dynamics of the Kepler-221 exoplanet system, working asynchronously.

  • Developed a complete Transit Timing Variation (TTV) analysis pipeline, including period detection via BLS periodogram and multi-start sinusoidal fitting.
