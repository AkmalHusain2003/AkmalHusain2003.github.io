I am a Computational Scientist and Astrophysicist holding an M.Sc. in Computational Science and a B.Sc. in Astronomy, both from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB). My primary research identity is as a statistician and machine learning practitioner with domain expertise in astrophysics — I apply statistical methods and machine learning, both supervised and unsupervised, to extract reliable structure from large-scale, high-dimensional datasets.

My Master's research developed ENTRAP, a hybrid algorithm combining density-based clustering with topological data analysis to identify open star clusters in Gaia DR3 data. I am currently undertaking a research internship at ASPIRE, Anton Pannekoek Institute for Astronomy, University of Amsterdam, building an N-body simulation pipeline to study the dynamics of the Kepler-221 exoplanetary system.

I hold rigor derived from first principles above all else before implementation — an approach I apply consistently, both in mathematical derivation and in writing scientific code that is accurate and verifiable.

Research Intern — ASPIRE Program

Anton Pannekoek Institute for Astronomy, University of Amsterdam · Supervised by Dr. T. C. N. Boekholt

Building an N-body simulation pipeline (AMUSE/Huayno) to investigate the dynamics of the Kepler-221 exoplanetary system, remotely.

  • Developed a full Transit Timing Variation (TTV) analysis pipeline, covering period detection via BLS periodogram and multi-start sinusoidal fitting.
  • Corrected simulation initial conditions using the full two-body Kepler's Third Law, verified to a numerical precision of e ~ 10⁻¹⁶.
  • Diagnosed and fixed astropy data-type incompatibilities in the analysis pipeline, and derived a transit-duration estimation function from first geometric principles.

2026 Accepted

A Quasar–Companion System Without AGN Outflow at z ∼ 6: The Case of PSO J083+11

Muhammad Akmal Husain, Irham Taufik Andika, Mochamad Ikbal Arifyanto · arXiv:2604.22559

Analysis of ALMA observations of the [C II] 158 μm emission line and far-infrared (FIR) continuum toward the quasar-companion system PSO J083.8371+11.8482 at z = 6.34, testing the impact of AGN activity on the companion galaxy during the cosmic reionization era. No morphological or kinematic evidence of AGN-driven outflow was found in either system; the result is interpreted as a pre-outflow accretion phase.
